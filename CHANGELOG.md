# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Notes with a subject and body, account-wide or character-only.
- Notes list with search and drag-and-drop reordering; resizable, lockable windows.
- Per-window border and opacity, window layer, and which side the note opens on.
- Optional per-character settings.
- Shift-click items, quests and other links into notes, with hover tooltips.
- Sticky notes that ignore Escape and reopen after a reload or relog.
- Optional "open on login".
